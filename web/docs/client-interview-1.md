## Extracted requirements (for UCMS)

### Membership

- Membership fee: **₱300** (one-time, on top of capital)
- Orientation: **required, onsite** (coop overview + duties/responsibilities)
- Qualification: faculty members; member types:
  - **Regular** — faculty
  - **Associate** — retired teachers who were members while in service
  - **Extension** — one family member (husband or child) of a member, **one only**
- Capital: new member **₱15,000** minimum, existing member **₱20,000** minimum
- No ceiling on capital — **staggered payments** allowed
- "Qualified member" only after full capital is paid

### Loans

- **6 types:** Salary/Regular, Education, Transportation/Vehicle, Gadget, Business, Emergency
- **Interest: 1.5%** (all types), **2.0%** (business loan only)
- **Method: diminishing** (interest on declining balance) — currently computed manually
- **Ceiling: 2× capital share** (₱100k capital → ₱200k loanable); overall max **₱150k–200k**
- **Max 2 concurrent loans** (regular + business allowed together, subject to committee approval)
- **Emergency loan:** max **₱20,000**, available *on top of* regular loan, ~2 months to pay (max 6 months)
- **Co-makers:** 1 if loan < ₱100,000; 2 if large amount
- **New members:** wait **2 months** + attend **financial literacy seminar (onsite)** before first loan
- **First loan: 80% only**; after full payment of first loan → may borrow up to 2× capital
- **Requirements:** valid ID (bank requirement) always; **salary proof** for large loans; **checking account** + salary proof for business loan
- **Approval:** committee ("subject for approval ng committee")
- **Before release:** coop computes remaining balance — shortfall from previous months may be deducted / rolled into the new loan
- **Release:** cash (if available) or cheque (Landbank) — member must come **in person** at release
- **Repayment channels:** ATM deduction (surrender optional but advised), **GCash**, coop counter, cheque
- **Delinquent = 6 months unpaid** → letter + text reminders
- **Penalties:** exist ("may computation") but the formula was **not given** — open question

### Registration / App

- **No pure online registration** — face-to-face still required (orientation, approval, membership fee, initial capital)
- **App pre-fill then send = accepted** ✅ (matches the UCMS Applications design)
- Data needed for **BIR** and **PAG-IBIG** (basic info + IDs)
- **Digital signature** on loan application = optional future feature
